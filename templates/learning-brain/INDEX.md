# My learning brain

Start with one subject. Fill in the four notes below, then use `skills/build-learning-board/SKILL.md`.

1. brief.md — what the learner will be able to do.
2. audience.md — who they are and what constraints matter.
3. knowledge.md — the approved source knowledge and examples.
4. quality.md — what a good learning asset must do and who reviews it.

Ask the AI coding assistant to create structured board.json content from these notes, then build and preview the board. Keep the source links visible. Missing knowledge should produce a question, not an invented answer.
