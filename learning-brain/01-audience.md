# The audience

Newly promoted shift managers at fictional Northstar Services. They were strong individual contributors; they may feel expected to have all the answers. Their first one-to-ones must fit around busy shifts.

Use direct, kind language. Give them a realistic line to respond to. Do not assume prior management training. Make the learning work on a phone and on a shared screen.

Constraint: ten minutes for this activity. Keep optional deeper detail out of the main map. Use short labels in the diagram and reveal explanation one step at a time.

Owner: fictional learning lead. Review: before the first team pilot.
