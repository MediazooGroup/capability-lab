# From a diagram to something you can learn with

## First draft
Three definitions: Ask. Listen. Agree. It summarises the framework but gives the learner no decision or rehearsal.

## Review against the brief
The brief asks for an observable conversation behaviour. Reading three definitions does not demonstrate it. The quality note requires explanatory feedback and a chance to practise.

## Refinement instruction
“Keep the Ask, Listen, Agree map. Add Sam's situation from 03-scenarios.md, three plausible responses, an explanation for every choice and a three-minute paired rehearsal. End with one action the learner can take away. Keep sources visible.”

## Current output
The refined board adds the decision, feedback, rehearsal and action note. The first-draft map remains available in the app to make this design change inspectable. These are two deliberately authored versions demonstrating the review pattern, not a live AI generation or a claim of learner-testing results.
