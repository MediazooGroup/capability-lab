# The brief

Teach: a useful first one-to-one for a newly promoted manager.

By the end, the learner can ask one open question, reflect what they heard before advising, and agree an action with an owner and follow-up date.

Output: an interactive Excalidraw learning dashboard with a whole-map view, six click-through stops, a realistic decision, feedback, a timed paired rehearsal and a downloadable action note. Include the editable `.excalidraw` file and a web version people can open from a QR code.

Time: ten minutes of learning. The board is also explorable without a facilitator. On a phone, the learning panel must remain readable even when the entire map is too small to read.

Owner: learning-design reviewer for this fictional example. Status: demonstration, ready for a small reviewed pilot; no claim of measured effectiveness.
