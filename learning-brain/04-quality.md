# Quality before launch

- The learner practises Ask, Listen and Agree, not just recalls definitions.
- Every design decision can be traced to the brief, audience, framework or scenario note.
- Fictional examples and the limits of the framework remain clear.
- A wrong choice receives an explanation and a chance to try again.
- A correct choice receives a reason, not just a green tick.
- The main map stays readable when zoomed to a stop; keyboard and phone users can use the same learning content in the panel.
- The learner can leave with one next action.
- The board opens, links work, downloads work and the QR points at the launched board.
- A subject owner checks accuracy and a learner pilot tests understanding before an organisation treats the asset as approved training.

For the workshop example, the build checks are recorded in BUILD-RECEIPT.md. A real learner pilot has not been run.
