# Ask, Listen, Agree

An illustrative framework created for this learning example.

## Ask
Start with one open question about the colleague's work: “What is getting in the way of a good week?” Avoid a leading question that supplies the answer.

## Listen
Let the person finish. Reflect back what you heard and check your understanding: “It sounds as if the priorities keep changing. Have I understood that?” Do not immediately prescribe a solution.

## Agree
Decide one next action together. Make the action, its owner and the follow-up date explicit. A shared next step is more useful than a vague promise to “communicate better”.

Use this for a routine developmental conversation. It is not a substitute for the organisation's policies or specialist procedures.
