# The practice

All people and situations in this note are fictional.

## Decision
Sam says: “Every shift the priorities change. I feel like I am always behind.”

A. “Try making a better to-do list.” — Advice arrives before you understand the work. Ask what is happening first.
B. “Which change has made the biggest difference this week?” — This invites a concrete example without assuming the cause. Ask, then listen and reflect back what you heard.
C. “We are all busy; it will settle down.” — This closes the conversation. Acknowledge the concern and ask an open question.

B is the strongest opening for this fictional situation. Context can change a good response; the reason matters more than memorising a letter.

## Paired rehearsal: three minutes
60 seconds: manager asks an open question; colleague replies as Sam.
60 seconds: manager reflects what they heard, checks it, then explores one possible action.
60 seconds: agree one action, an owner and a follow-up date. Observer checks all three behaviours.

## Model next step
“I will clarify tomorrow's priorities before the shift starts. Sam will flag conflicting requests. We will check how that worked on Friday.” This is illustrative, not a required policy.

## Ten-minute path
Orient 1 minute; Ask 1 minute; Listen 1 minute; Agree 1 minute; Try Sam's scenario 2 minutes; paired rehearsal and action note 4 minutes.
