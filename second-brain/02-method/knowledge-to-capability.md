# Knowledge becomes capability

A second brain is a maintained collection of useful knowledge you can retrieve, connect and use. For an L&D team, begin with a recurring job and its approved sources: audience needs, frameworks, brand guidance, policies and examples of good work.

A prompt asks for an output. A reusable skill describes when it runs, the inputs it needs, the steps it follows, the output it produces and the checks it must pass. Save these instructions as readable text, alongside relevant examples and tests. A skill is a recipe. The brain supplies the ingredients and context.

The loop: knowledge, instructions, skill, human check, useful work. Save what the review teaches you back into the knowledge and instructions. Repetition alone does not improve a system; the feedback has to change it.

The leadership unit is one valuable task with an owner, a baseline and a review date. Start with low-risk work that recurs and has a clear quality standard. Measure quality and useful behaviour alongside time, so faster drafting does not hide extra review effort.

Engagement: a learning asset addresses a real decision the audience faces. Visibility: leaders see what changed and what evidence supports it. Impact: compare the pilot with a baseline using a relevant work measure. Do not confuse attendance, AI usage or output volume with business outcomes.

Tool portability: the knowledge, instructions and review criteria can travel as files or pasted text. Copilot, Claude and Gemini have different upload, retrieval and execution features, which also vary by account. Use the approved interface available to you. Never promise identical outputs or universal support for SKILL.md auto-discovery.
