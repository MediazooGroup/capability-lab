# Keep the knowledge useful

Begin with one problem you return to. Capture raw material into an inbox. Preserve the original, then distil decisions, owners, dates and evidence into linked notes. Mark who owns a note, where it came from and when it needs review.

Each week, inspect new material and proposed changes. Update existing notes, record contradictions and retire superseded guidance. Keep a short change log. A source line makes a statement traceable, but does not prove it is correct. A human checks it against the original.

You can use projects, areas, resources and archives for a personal brain. A team brain can organise by strategy, delivery, product, governance and brand. The folder names matter less than finding the right approved source for the job. Start small.

Keep source permissions in the storage system. Metadata such as public, restricted or private is a reminder, not an access control. Use synthetic or public examples in open events. A review gate must be able to stop publication.
