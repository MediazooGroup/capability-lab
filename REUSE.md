# Reuse

The original workshop text, source notes, build skills and application code authored for this kit may be copied and adapted for your own learning assets. Keep a reference to the original example when sharing a derivative kit.

Finer Vision and Mediazoo names, logos and John Gordon’s portrait identify the original speaker and workshop. Replace them with your own identity in adaptations; they do not imply endorsement. The portrait comes from Mediazoo’s public announcement of John’s appointment.

Third-party software remains under its own licence, including Excalidraw (MIT) and its bundled font licences. The lockfile records dependencies; installed packages include their notices. Do not remove required notices when redistributing a built application. The source and built asset include THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt.

The Northstar scenario and illustrative framework are educational examples, not a representation of a real organisation’s policy or proof of learning effectiveness.
