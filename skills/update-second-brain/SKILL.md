# Update second brain

Read `../../second-brain/INDEX.md` and the relevant existing notes. Determine which new material is allowed in this brain. Preserve each original input. Summarise the useful decisions, evidence and questions, link them to existing notes and identify the source and review owner.

When guidance conflicts, keep the conflict visible until an authoritative source or the owner resolves it. A new timestamp alone does not make an unsupported claim true. Update the index and change log after the edit. Ask before deleting originals or publishing restricted material. For normal local updates already requested, proceed without adding an approval ritual.

Return the changed notes and a short log of what changed and why. Check that every new link resolves and that no private source has entered a public kit.
